Logo Guidelines

Logo Mark & Construction

The official Sore emblem embodies acoustic waveforms and the serene solar disc at dusk. Ensure all implementations strictly adhere to clear space rules and background contrast.

Clear Space = 0.5X
Master Logo with Sunset Aura Halo & Soundmark

Construction Specifications

The Sore monogram is engineered with geometric precision and subtle curve transitions.

  • Clear Space: Maintain a minimum clear space of 0.5X around the perimeter of the mark.
  • Minimum Digital Size: 24×24 px for UI icons, 48×48 px for bot avatars.
  • Background Pairing: Always place on dark carbon/obsidian (#090A0D) or high-contrast backgrounds.

Do's & Don'ts (Usage Standards)

✓ DO
Use Official Variants
Always render official vector or high-resolution PNG marks on dark or transparent backgrounds.
✗ DON'T
Do Not Recolor to Blue or Purple
The official Sore identity is Sunset Rose & Carbon. Never tint or recolor to blue, purple, or indigo.
✗ DON'T
Do Not Stretch or Distort
Always preserve the 1:1 square aspect ratio. Never skew, squash, or deform the logo.
✗ DON'T
Do Not Rotate or Tilt
Keep the mark horizontally aligned. Never tilt, slant, or rotate the orientation.

Voice & Tone

Brand Voice & Writing Guidelines

Sore's communication principles are crafted to be warm, soothing, responsive, and completely free from technical clutter.

Tone 01

Warm & Soothing

Communicate with welcoming warmth like coffee at golden hour. Avoid cold or robotic error codes; always suggest an immediate solution if track playback fails.

Tone 02

Crisp & Responsive

Acknowledge commands promptly and concisely. Display song titles, artists, and playback timers clearly without cluttered chat spam.

Tone 03

Respectful & Inclusive

Celebrate all music genres and communities without bias. Maintain uniform button layouts and command syntax so new listeners and server admins feel right at home.
